# WYNIX

WYNIX started as a fork of **Mac Steam Setup** by heung115
(https://github.com/heung115/mac-steam-setup), released under the MIT License.
The original copyright and license notice are kept in `LICENSE`, as MIT requires.

WYNIX adds its own work on top: bottles and per-bottle settings, game store installers,
Install from File, Optimize, Boost, the cover-art game library, the first-run tour,
Help, the WYNIX brand and icon, and the website.

Third-party components are downloaded from their upstream releases and remain under
their own licenses. See [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for the
component-by-component license and source URLs, including Wine (LGPL-2.1), DXMT 0.74
(MIT), DXVK, MoltenVK, Sikarugir, Whisky Libraries 4.8.3, and Apple's D3DMetal/GPTK
license.
