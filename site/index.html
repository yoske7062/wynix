Which games work?

WYNIX targets DirectX 11 and 12 games on Steam, and single player games are the safest bet. Results vary by game and by Mac, so try yours: it's free, and you'll know in a few minutes.

Fortnite, Valorant, Apex, Call of Duty?

No. Games with kernel level anti-cheat refuse to start outside Windows. That's true for every Wine-based tool on Mac, paid or free.

My Mac says it can't verify the app. Is it safe?

Mac says the app isn't safe? Normal. Here's the fix.

WYNIX isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS shows "Apple could not verify WYNIX is free of malware" the first time. Nothing is wrong with your download. Pick one of these, you only do it once.

Option 1 System Settings

  1. Open WYNIX once and click Done on the warning.
  2. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down and click Open Anyway next to WYNIX.
  4. Confirm with your password. Done.

Option 2 Terminal, 5 seconds

Move WYNIX to Applications, open Terminal and paste this:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/WYNIX.app

This removes the "downloaded from the internet" flag from WYNIX only. Nothing else on your Mac changes.
