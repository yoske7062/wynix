Privacy

WYNIX doesn't need an account and doesn't know who you are. Here's everything that leaves your Mac.

Update checks (the app)

When WYNIX opens, and every 6 hours while open, it asks our server if there's a new version. That request includes a random install ID (made up on your Mac the first time it runs, not linked to you) and the app version number. We use it only to count how many people use WYNIX and which versions are in use. The app does not send your name, email, files, list of games, or anything about what you play.

Downloads and the website

  • When you download WYNIX we count the download and the country it came from (from our hosting provider's network data). We don't store your IP address. To stop abuse (someone inflating the counter in a loop), we keep a one-way hash of it for at most one hour, then it's deleted.
  • The website uses Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page visits without cookies.

What third parties see

  • Our website and counters are hosted on Vercel, and the counts are stored with Upstash. They process requests as part of providing that service.
  • Steam, Epic, GOG, Battle.net, EA, Ubisoft and any other store or program you install run under their own privacy policies. WYNIX downloads them from their official sources and never sees your logins.
  • Donations are handled entirely by Ko-fi and its payment providers (for example PayPal). We see the name and message you choose to share on Ko-fi, never your payment details.

Keeping and deleting data

Counts are kept as totals and per-day numbers. Because the install ID is random and not linked to you, we can't look you up. To stop update checks, quit WYNIX or remove it. If you want any supporter information we hold removed (like your name on the Supporters list), contact us.
