Will my game run?

Which games work?

Most DirectX 11 and DirectX 12 games on Steam. Single player and most online games run fine. Games that use kernel level anti-cheat won't start outside Windows, so some competitive shooters are off the table for now.

What Mac do I need?

Any Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) on macOS 14 or later. More GPU cores means higher settings, but an M1 MacBook Air will happily run plenty of games.

Free? What's the catch?

There isn't one. No ads, no trial, no account with us. You sign in to Steam, not to WYNIX.

Will it slow down or heat up my Mac?

WYNIX only runs while you play. Steam is kept hidden in the background, Game Mode quiets other apps, and the 60 FPS cap stops your Mac from working harder than it needs to.

Do my saves and achievements work?

Yes. It's the real Steam client, so cloud saves, achievements and friends all work as usual.
