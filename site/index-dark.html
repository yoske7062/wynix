FAQ
Is it really free?

Yes. WYNIX is free and open source. No trial, no subscription.

Do I need Windows?

No. WYNIX runs Windows games directly on macOS. Nothing to install or license.

Which Macs are supported?

Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 and newer) running a recent version of macOS.

Does every game work?

Most do. Games with kernel level anti-cheat cannot run outside Windows, so some online shooters will not start.

Ready to play on your Mac?
